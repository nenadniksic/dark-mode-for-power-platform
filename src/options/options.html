Canvas Studio Dark Mode

Settings sync with your browser profile. Changes apply instantly to open Studio tabs.

General
Appearance“Follow system” turns dark mode on only while your OS uses a dark colour scheme.
Look
SurfaceBase lightness of panels and bars. Dark matches Fluent UI’s dark theme.
Where

Published apps and the player (apps.powerapps.com) are never touched, and inside Studio the canvas that shows your app is rendered exactly as designed. Windows High Contrast mode takes precedence over this theme.

About

Version 1.0.0. This extension stores only its settings, collects nothing, and makes no network requests other than fetching Studio's own stylesheets.
