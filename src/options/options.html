Dark Mode for Power Platform

Settings sync with your browser profile. Changes apply instantly to open Studio tabs.

General
Appearance“Follow system” turns dark mode on only while your OS uses a dark colour scheme. “Sunset to sunrise” and “Custom hours” switch it on and off by the clock. Each choice keeps its own hours and location while you try another.
Dark hoursDark mode turns on at the first time and off at the second, by this computer’s clock. Overnight hours are fine.

Where

Published apps and the player (apps.powerapps.com) are never touched, and inside Studio the canvas that shows your app is rendered exactly as designed. Each product keeps its own accent colour: purple in Power Apps, blue in Power Automate. Windows High Contrast mode takes precedence over this theme.
